    Common questions

    What does the free preview actually give me?

    A real draft written from your intake and the funder's guidelines, cut off partway through. It shows you the tone and the argument before you spend anything.

    What changes when I pay?

    Paid drafts are written by Claude and run 700 to 900 words with a full structure: need, funder fit, program, evidence, budget use, and a closing ask. You can also edit, copy, download, and rewrite any earlier preview in full.

    What if my program doesn't have hard outcomes data?

    Most small nonprofits don't. It works with what you have — intake counts, staff observations, past program numbers — and writes honestly instead of inventing statistics.

    Do you guarantee funding?

    No one honestly can. What you get is a draft matched to that funder's language and priorities, fast.

    How is this different from a general AI chatbot?

    Grant-writing practice is applied automatically, your organization's details carry across drafts, and everything stays in one place. You never write a prompt.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Monthly plans cancel from the Account tab and stay active until the end of the period. Lifetime access does not expire.
